QML-01: Delay-Risk-Aware Fleet Dispatch for Contract Logistics

Problem. A contract logistics operator has finite vehicles, vehicle-specific capacities, delivery
windows and contractual commitments. Traffic and service-time uncertainty can make a
cheap-looking route fail its SLA. Produce vehicle routes that are operationally feasible and
minimize actual transport/labor expense together with estimated service-failure exposure.

Deliver. Exact vehicle-to-route plan, ordered stops, ETA/risk for each service commitment and
total financial exposure. Evaluate SLA violations, valid coverage, cost, prediction calibration,
solver quality and runtime.

8. JUDGEMENT RUBRICS: QpiAI TECHNOLOGY METRIC

The following scoring criteria shall be used for scoring the use of QpiAI technologies in the
submissions, and shall be incorporated into the judgement rubrics accordingly. The 20 points
score assumes a 100 points-based scoring system; the scaling shall be adjusted as applicable.

A. USE OF QPIAI TECHNOLOGIES — 20 points.

Judges must score ACTUAL invocation, relevance, correctness and measured integration of
QpiAI technology, not mentions in slides or a decorative circuit.

A1. Correct, meaningful QpiAI product choice and domain integration — 5 points.
Does the selected QpiAI component address a genuine computation or domain decision? Are
inputs and outputs materially connected to the validated business outcome?

A2. Technical formulation and correct use of features — 10 points.
QpiAI QCloud and associated technical correctness — 5 points (50%).
QpiAI Opt and associated technical correctness — 5 points (50%).

A3. Working QpiAI implementation with recorded execution evidence — 5 points.
Working SDK and solver runs, recorded outputs and reproducibility.
